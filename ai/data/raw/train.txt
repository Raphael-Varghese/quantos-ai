The artificial intelligence was created to learn from examples.

Neural networks are mathematical models that learn patterns from data.

A transformer is a neural network architecture based on attention.

Machine learning allows computers to learn relationships between inputs and outputs.

The model reads a sequence of tokens and predicts what token should come next.

Artificial intelligence can be used to understand language, generate text, analyze information, and solve problems.

The computer does not understand language in the same way that a human does. Instead, the neural network learns statistical patterns in its training data.

Training a neural network involves making predictions, calculating an error, and adjusting the model's parameters to reduce that error.

Deep learning uses many layers of neural network computations.

Attention allows a model to determine which parts of a sequence are important when processing another part of the sequence.
